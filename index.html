⚠️ Pegadinhas da Escolinha

  1. Do you…? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't. · Does he…? → Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't. Não misture!
  2. Escreva does com e: d·o·e·s (e não "dos").
  3. Instrumento vem com the (play the piano); jogo e esporte vêm sem (play soccer, play video games, play board games).
  4. I (eu) é sempre maiúsculo: Yes, I do. (com vírgula depois do Yes).
  5. Wednesday = Wed·nes·day. Os dias sempre com letra maiúscula.
  6. Saturday × Sunday: os dois começam com S! A banda do Evan toca no Sunday.
  7. Orquestra do Diego: violin, recorder, piano. Banda do Evan: electric guitar, keyboard, drums (e o Evan canta).
  8. and = e · but = mas · too = também (no fim da frase).
  9. oa: boat, coat, goat · ow: row, low, yellow (e não "yelloy").
  10. Falando de outra pessoa, o verbo ganha s: I play → Sarah plays.

🙋 Do e Does em 4 ideias

VOCÊ (you) / EU (I)ELE (he)
❓ PerguntaDo you play the piano? Does he play the piano?
👍 SimYes, I do. Yes, he does.
👎 NãoNo, I don't. No, he doesn't.
📢 AfirmativaI play soccer. Sarah plays the recorder.
🎹 Instrumento com the: play the piano, the drums, the violin · ⚽ Jogo e esporte sem the: play soccer, video games, board games.
🔁 on + dia com s = toda semana: Do you go to school on Sundays? · Does he paint on Sundays?

🔤 Palavras importantes

🔗 and · but · too

➕ and = e
on Wednesdays and Fridays
↔️ but = mas
She plays the trumpet, but she prefers the recorder.
🙌 too = também
Diego likes yoga, too.
